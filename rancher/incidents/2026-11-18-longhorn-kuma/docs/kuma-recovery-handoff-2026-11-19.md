## Safety and scope for future use

No formatting, forced detach, indiscriminate shim kill or automatic -y repair
was used. Filesystem recovery was tested on a verified working image before
original-volume repair. DB/WAL/SHM stayed together; immutable main-file checking
would miss WAL-only committed transactions and was not used.

`actions.jsonl` and `cap-changes.jsonl` record requested versus confirmed limits,
worker identity, timestamps, reason, pressure and outcome. One working-copy
attempt stopped at local full PSI 2.25 and confirmed cap 1 MiB/s -> 0; its
partial image is unverified. The subsequent attempt passed. Last confirmed
extraction cap returned to zero at 19:49:51 UTC on 18 November. Restoration
pressure pauses are not transfer-cap reductions. Future cap changes require
the same records and live-worker confirmation.

ordinary application/Longhorn I/O and a progressing recurring backup were
observed at different times; do not turn temporal overlap into a causal claim.
Stopping applications cluster-wide is the required PVC migration mitigation,
not a proven hardware repair. Keep essential infrastructure/DNS/Longhorn running.

Scripts have exact incident paths/UIDs/IDs and attempt markers. They are incident
tools, not generic automation: review/adapt guards and create fresh baselines
for another incident. Do not rerun completed shutdown, source repair, copies or
